Data definitions & simulation assumptions

All source values come from the supplied HTML prototype. Production and transportation are planning snapshots; the demand-window filter controls the weekly chart and forecast accuracy. The plant filter controls the selected planning snapshot and lane rows. Plant-specific demand series are illustrative allocations of the network series using each plant’s share of the prototype’s plant forecast totals; these are not observed plant orders.

The +20% scenario increases the latest week’s confirmed order index by 20%, and applies a 20% stress assumption to planned production and planned loads across the selected scope. Loads are rounded up to whole loads; production capacity and contracted carrier capacity remain fixed. The scenario applies to all plants so it remains consistent when switching plant filters. Clicking the scenario again does not compound it. Reset restores all filters and source values.

Lane coverage uses the smaller of planned loads and contracted capacity for each lane, so surplus on one lane does not hide a gap on another. Spot-market dependency represents uncovered capacity that may require review, not an actual spot-market purchase. Plant transport totals and lane totals are separate illustrative snapshots in the original prototype; they are not reconciled totals.

Prototype concept for case competition · All figures and events are synthetic examples, not Ball Corporation data or validated targets.
